Levels of care

The right level, not the biggest one.

Placement is set by an ASAM assessment and reviewed as treatment goes on. Our outpatient levels are running now in Easton; the inpatient levels open in Baltimore once accreditation and licensure are complete.

ASAM Program Level of care Who it fits Typical length
3.5 P2H Recovery
Baltimore — opening		 High-intensity residential Needs 24-hour structure; prior relapse, unsafe environment, or high-acuity co-occurring needs 21–35 days
3.1 P2H Recovery
Baltimore — opening		 Low-intensity residential Stable in treatment but not yet stable at home; building routine, work, and recovery supports 30–90 days
2.1 P2H Wellness
Easton — open		 Intensive outpatient Living at home and working or caregiving; needs 9+ clinical hours a week 8–12 weeks
1.0 P2H Wellness
Easton — open		 Outpatient Maintaining recovery with weekly therapy and medication management Ongoing
0.5 P2H Wellness
Easton — open		 Early intervention Risky use without a diagnosis yet — education, screening, and short-term counseling 4–8 sessions

Lengths of stay are typical ranges, not limits. Inpatient levels (3.5 and 3.1) are pending accreditation and Maryland licensure and are not yet admitting; call and we'll place you with a program we trust in the meantime.

What treatment actually looks like

IndividualWeekly one-to-one therapy with the same counselor throughout your stay.
GroupCBT and DBT-informed skills groups, relapse prevention, and trauma-aware process work.
MedicationBuprenorphine, naltrexone, and coordination with methadone providers where appropriate.
FamilyFamily sessions and education, scheduled around the people who need to be there.
AftercareA written step-down plan and a warm handoff — usually to our own outpatient team.
