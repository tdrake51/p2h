What we mean by whatever it takes

Evidence-based, not ideological

CBT, DBT-informed skills, trauma-focused therapy, and medication for addiction when it's indicated. We don't withhold medication to make a point.

A plan built for one person

Placement comes from an ASAM assessment, then gets revisited as things change — not fixed to a 28-day template on day one.

Experience behind the name

P2H was built by clinicians and operators who have run behavioral health programs for years, in these communities.

Families in the room

Family sessions and education are part of treatment, because the people at home are part of what happens after discharge.
