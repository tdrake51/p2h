Four levels, one screen

Partial hospitalization is the level our Easton program does not run. It is the difference between stepping down from a hospital or a residential bed into real support, and stepping down into a weekly appointment.

New at P2HASAM 2.5

Partial hospitalization

Around 20 clinical hours a week, most of them in the morning block, for people who need more than an evening group but can be safe at home overnight. Common after a hospital stay, after residential treatment, or instead of one when the risk is manageable.

How it works

What you actually need to start

Less than people expect. If you can make a video call, you can do this. If your connection drops, we finish on the phone and nobody loses the hour.

A deviceA phone, tablet, or computer with a camera. We send a link by text or email — no app store account, no software to buy.
A private hourSomewhere you can speak without being overheard. A parked car counts. If you truly have nowhere, tell us at intake and we will work on it with you.
PennsylvaniaYou need to be physically in the state during each session. That is a licensing rule, not a preference, and we check it.
A safety planAt intake we record where you will usually be and the crisis resources closest to you, so help can reach the right address if it is ever needed.
Local labsDrug screening and any lab work are arranged near you, not at our office — because we do not have one.

When a screen is the right room — and when it isn't

Telehealth works well when

A job, a class, or custody makes a daytime commute impossible
You live an hour or more from the nearest program that has an opening
You have transportation that is unreliable, or none
Walking into a building in your own town is the thing stopping you
You are stepping down from inpatient and want to keep the intensity up at home

You need something else first when

You are in withdrawal or need medically monitored detox
A medical or psychiatric condition is unstable right now
You are actively suicidal or at immediate risk — call or text 988
Staying where you are living makes recovery unrealistic

The assessment sorts this out honestly. If telehealth is not the right level, we say so and help you find the one that is.

Paying for it

Cost will not be the reason you stop

These are commitments we have put in writing as part of our Pennsylvania application, not a slogan.

Medical Assistance. We are pursuing Pennsylvania Medical Assistance enrollment, and we help you apply at intake if you are eligible and not enrolled.
A sliding fee scale. Based on household income and size, applied the same way for everyone.
Coverage lapses. If your coverage drops mid-treatment, treatment continues while we sort it out.
Reserved slots. A share of our capacity is held for people with no funding at all.
No discharge for inability to pay. Plainly: we do not discharge people because of a balance.
